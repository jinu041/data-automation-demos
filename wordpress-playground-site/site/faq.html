Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about booking, timing, warranty and payment. Can't find yours? Call (555) 555-0147.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is best, so we can order parts and give you a firm pickup time. For warning lights, flat tires or brake noise, call us and we will fit you in as soon as we can.

How long will my car take?

Most services and brake jobs are done the same day if you drop the car off before 9:00 am. We give you a pickup time at drop-off and text you if it changes.

Will you do any work without asking me?

No. We inspect the car, send a written quote and wait for your OK. If we find something else during the job, we call you before doing anything about it.

Which cars do you work on?

Most cars, SUVs and light trucks, including hybrids. We do not work on motorcycles or heavy commercial vehicles.

What warranty do I get?

Parts and labor on every repair are covered for 12 months or 12,000 miles, whichever comes first. Your invoice is your warranty record.

Can I wait while you work?

Yes, for jobs that take about an hour, such as oil changes and tire rotations. The waiting room has coffee and Wi-Fi.

How do I pay?

By card or cash when you pick up the car. You pay only for the work you approved.

Call (555) 555-0147 or send us a message. We reply within one working day.
